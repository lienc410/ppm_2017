The query for swap 2yr and 10yr:

select m.TickerName, d.asOfDate, d.SeriesNumValue
    from TimeSeriesMeta m, TimeSeries d
    where m.TimeSeriesMetaId = d.TimeSeriesMetaId
    and m.StatusCode = 'ACTIVE'
	and m.source = 'YIELDBOOK'
	and d.AsOfDate <= '20131231'
	and m.TickerName in ('2yr Swap Rate', '10yr Swap Rate')
	and m.SeriesType = 'Rate'
	and m.Category = 'SWAPRATE'

The query for swation vol 3y7y:

select m.TickerName, d.asOfDate, m.SeriesType, d.SeriesNumValue
    from TimeSeriesMeta m, TimeSeries d
    where m.TimeSeriesMetaId = d.TimeSeriesMetaId
    and m.StatusCode = 'ACTIVE'
	and m.source = 'YIELDBOOK'
	and d.AsOfDate <= '20150202'
	and m.TickerName = '3y7y_ATM'
	and m.SeriesType = 'Normalized_Volatility'
	and m.Category = 'Rate'
